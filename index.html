KNOW THE RISKS

Common Financial Cyber Threats

Understanding common attacks can help you recognise suspicious activity before it becomes a problem.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords or financial information.

HIGH RISK
🎭

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information or approving fraudulent transactions.

HIGH RISK
🆔

Identity Theft

Criminals may use stolen personal information to impersonate you or access financial services.

MEDIUM RISK
🦠

Malware

Malicious software can steal information, monitor activity or damage devices used for online banking.

HIGH RISK

SIMPLE HABITS

Seven Ways to Stay Safer

01

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords and avoid using easily guessed personal information.

02

Enable MFA

Add an additional verification step to protect your important accounts.

03

Think Before Clicking

Check unexpected links and messages carefully before interacting with them.

04

Monitor Transactions

Review your account regularly and report suspicious transactions quickly.

05

Update Devices

Install security updates for your operating system and applications.

06

Protect Personal Data

Avoid sharing sensitive information through unverified channels.

07

Act Quickly

Contact your bank immediately if you suspect your account has been compromised.
